# Lab 2 - Configure Microsoft Sentinel Roles, Retention, Workbooks, SOC Optimization, and Data Tiers

## Objective

Configure or design Microsoft Sentinel roles, data retention, workbooks, platform optimization, SOC optimization recommendations, and table tiers.

## Scenario

Contoso is deploying Sentinel and needs a clear platform configuration before ingesting all data sources.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Assign Sentinel roles

Create:

| Role | Assigned group | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|----------------|

Include reader, responder, contributor, and playbook operator concepts.

### Step 2 - Plan data retention

Create:

| Table or data type | Retention | Tier | Reason |
|--------------------|-----------|------|--------|

Include Analytics, Data lake, and XDR tier concepts.

### Step 3 - Design workbooks

Plan workbooks for:

- Incident overview
- Connector health
- Endpoint security
- Identity risk
- Hunting results

### Step 4 - Review optimization

Document:

- Cost drivers
- Table usage
- Ingestion volume
- SOC optimization recommendation
- Query performance concern

### Step 5 - Create platform checklist

Include workspace, permissions, retention, connector health, workbooks, automation, and metrics.

## Deliverable

Submit role plan, retention table, workbook plan, optimization notes, and platform checklist.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why should Sentinel roles be scoped?
2. What tradeoff exists between retention and cost?
3. What should a SOC workbook show?
