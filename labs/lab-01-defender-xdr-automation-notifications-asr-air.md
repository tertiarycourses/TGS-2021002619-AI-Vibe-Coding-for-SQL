# Lab 1 - Configure Defender XDR Automation, Notifications, ASR, AIR, and Attack Disruption

## Objective

Configure or design Microsoft Defender XDR automation, email and alert notifications, Defender for Endpoint advanced features, ASR rules, automated investigation and response, and automatic attack disruption.

## Scenario

Contoso analysts receive too many untuned alerts and respond manually to common endpoint threats.

## Tasks

### Step 1 - Configure notification plan

Create:

| Notification | Audience | Trigger | Action |
|--------------|----------|---------|--------|

Include incidents, actions, and threat analytics.

### Step 2 - Tune alerts

Document:

- Alert suppression use case
- Correlation benefit
- Severity review
- False positive handling

### Step 3 - Plan Defender for Endpoint settings

Create checklist for:

- Advanced features
- Rules settings
- Device groups
- Permissions
- Automation levels
- Custom data collection

### Step 4 - Design ASR policy

Create:

| ASR rule | Mode | Pilot group | Success metric |
|----------|------|-------------|----------------|

### Step 5 - Plan AIR and attack disruption

Document:

- Automated investigation scope
- Response action approval
- Attack disruption prerequisites
- Analyst review process

## Deliverable

Submit notification plan, alert tuning notes, endpoint settings checklist, ASR policy, and AIR/attack disruption plan.

## Checkpoint questions

1. Why should automation levels differ by device group?
2. What is the value of alert suppression?
3. Why should ASR rules be piloted?
